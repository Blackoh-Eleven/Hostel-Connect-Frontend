Students Deserve Clearer Hostel Prices.

Finding a hostel shouldn't feel like a guessing game. Students often have to depend on agents, word of mouth, or conflicting information just to find out how much accommodation actually costs.

What's the problem?

Hostel prices can be difficult for students to know before starting their search. This can make it harder to compare options, plan financially, and make informed decisions.

We believe students should have easier access to clear and transparent information about hostel prices and available accommodation.

What are we asking for?

We are asking for support for greater transparency around student accommodation prices in Malete.

🔒

Transparent Hostel Information

We want to work with the Students' Union and other relevant stakeholders to make reliable hostel price information easier for students to access.

👥

Better Access for Students

Students should be able to discover available hostels and compare accommodation options without unnecessary barriers.
