Important Notice

Hostel Connect is a platform designed to help students discover and connect with accommodation opportunities. Listings and information are provided by users, and Hostel Connect does not guarantee the accuracy, availability, condition, pricing, or suitability of any accommodation.

Users are currently responsible for independently verifying accommodation details and exercising appropriate caution before making payments or entering into any agreement. Never send money solely because of a listing or communication on the platform.

As Hostel Connect continues to grow, we are developing additional verification systems and safety measures designed to improve the reliability of listings and provide students with a more trusted accommodation experience.
